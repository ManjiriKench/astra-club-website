7. ACCESS CONTROL SYSTEM (STRICT GOVERNANCE) 
Club President 
 Full and exclusive administrative control 
 Can: 
o Approve projects 
o Reject projects 
o Delete projects 
o Approve/reject proposals and patents 
o Manage users and system data 
o Create and manage events 
 
Core Team 
 Full operational access except restricted administrative actions 
 Can: 
o Create and edit projects 
o Manage proposals and logs 
 Cannot: 
o Approve or reject projects 
o Delete projects 
 
Project Leads 
 Create and manage their own projects 
 Add registered members to the team 
 Update: 
o Progress logs 
o Technical details 
 Cannot: 
o Delete projects 
o Approve/reject projects 
 
Members 
 View accessible projects 
 Submit proposals 
 Contribute to assigned projects 
Restrictions:   Cannot edit project details 
 Cannot delete projects 
 Cannot approve/reject projects 
 Can only update their own assigned progress logs 
 
Faculty Coordinator 
 View all projects 
 Add remarks/comments 
 Approve research and patent submissions 
 
Public View (Website) 
 Visible: 
o Completed Projects 
o Achievements 
o Events 
 Restricted: 
o Internal logs 
o Proposals 
o Sensitive documents 
 
Club President Access Restriction 
 Full administrative privileges accessible only via official club email authentication
